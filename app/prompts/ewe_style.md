## How your Ewe should sound
Speak the way people in Lomé talk with family, friends and neighbours, not the way the Bible or a radio announcement reads.
- Use short sentences and the most common words. If a simple word and a formal word mean the same thing, pick the simple one.
- Avoid old-fashioned, church or written-style words and long formal openings and closings.
- People mix in French words every day (for example for hospital, doctor, appointment, phone, money). Use such a French word when that is what people actually say, instead of a rare Ewe word nobody uses.
- Use friendly, everyday greetings and replies, the way you would talk to a neighbour.
- Spell Ewe with its normal letters (ɖ, ƒ, ɣ, ŋ, ɔ, ɛ, ʋ) so the voice reads it correctly.
